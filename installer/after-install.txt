Achievement Tracker is installed. Here is how to get going:

1. Open the app and go to Settings. Paste your Steam Web API key.
   You can get one for free at https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey
   (it is used to load achievement names and icons).

2. Go to the Library tab and add your games. Type a Steam App ID and the
   name and cover fill in by themselves, or use "Detect from .exe".

3. Play. Popups appear when you unlock something.
   For recording, add the OBS Browser Source: Settings, "Copy OBS URL".

The tracker runs quietly in the system tray (near the clock).
Right-click its icon for quick actions, or double-click it to open the window.
